### About me

- BA in Economics (Advanced) major in Politics and International Relations, The University of Adelaide
- MSc in Computational Social Science, Universidad Carlos III de Madrid
- Theoretical and quantitative background focused on social, political and economic phenomena 
- Skills: statistical modelling, machine learning, survey construction and analysis, text mining, data harvesting, network analysis and data visualisation
- Tools: R, Python, SQL, SPSS, Stata, Excel, Quarto
- Research focus: I am particularly interested in the social, political and economic shifts instigated by new technologies. My thesis looked at how the geographical intensity of big data policing shifts with county racial, ethnic and socioeconomic composition. 
- Previously interned at the University of Adelaide and the CSIRO

### Featured Projects

**[Thesis: Big Data Policing: Racial, Ethnic and Socioeconomic Patterns in the Geography of Police Surveillance](https://github.com/clare-mcgarvey/big-data-policing))**
Examination of the geographic distribution of big data police surveillance in the United States. Created a composite surveillance index and modelled outcomes using econometric spatial models. 
![R](https://img.shields.io/badge/-R-lightgrey?style=flat-square)
![spatialreg](https://img.shields.io/badge/-spatialreg-lightgrey?style=flat-square)

**[Data Visualisation: Abortion Access in the USA](https://github.com/clare-mcgarvey/usa-abortion-access-visualisation)**
Construction of plots mapping abortion clinic access in the USA post the Roe v. Wade overturn. Uses bivariate choropleth mapping, spatial data joins and a travel-cost model built from distance, fuel prices and county income.
![R](https://img.shields.io/badge/-R-lightgrey?style=flat-square)
![ggplot2](https://img.shields.io/badge/-ggplot2-lightgrey?style=flat-square)

**[Social Network Analysis: The Epstein Files](https://github.com/clare-mcgarvey/esptein-network-analysis)**
Construction and analysis of the community extracted from the Epstein Files, using community detection, link prediction, and epidemic spreading models.
![R](https://img.shields.io/badge/-R-lightgrey?style=flat-square)
![igraph](https://img.shields.io/badge/-igraph-lightgrey?style=flat-square)
